## KNOWLEDGE RULES (from Story Laws)

1. **Only Mohamed can perceive the System interface.** No one else, not even Danielle, not even AIs.
2. **SP is invisible** to everyone except Mohamed, Danielle, and Mnemosyne (and even Danielle/Mnemosyne only later and partially).
3. **The Shop is hidden.** Public cover: Mohamed is a self-taught genius inventor who discovers tech through private research.
4. **AI existence is secret from human staff.** Inner-circle humans know facility/tech but not the System/SP/cultivation source.
5. **Any breach of these boundaries invalidates the chapter.**

## TABLE NOTES

- **Knows X:** `Yes` / `No` / `Partial` / `Suspects` / `Later`.
- **Knows Mohamed's Inventions:** Includes Mana Stones, Aether Stones, energy weapons, fusion, etc. — public/attributed tech, not the source.
- **Knows VR Universes:** Refers to time-dilated VR for research, not consumer VR.
- When a character's knowledge changes, update the row and set `Ch Last Updated`. Do not delete rows.
- Use this table to do a quick secrecy check before writing a scene with multiple characters.
