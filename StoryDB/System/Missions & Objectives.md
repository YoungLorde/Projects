## TABLE NOTES

- **Missions:** High-level story objectives. Add rows as Mohamed gains them, complete them, or abandon them.
- **Karma Seeds:** Foreshadowing hooks. Every seed must have a `Seeded Ch`, `Payoff Arc`, and `Status` (`Open`, `Realized`, `Abandoned`, `Reseeded`). When a seed pays off, update the `Realized Ch` column and change Status to `Realized`.
- **Dependencies:** Prior mission IDs that must be completed first.
- The Karma Board in `Memory/Story State.md` is a live view of `KSEED-*` rows with `Status = Open`.

---

> Append new missions and seeds as new rows. Use `Registry.md` to assign the next `MISS-` and `KSEED-` IDs.
