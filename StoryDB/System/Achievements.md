## TABLE NOTES

- **Status:** Pending / Unlocked / Obsolete / Secret.
- When an achievement is unlocked, update the `Ch Unlocked` column and change Status to `Unlocked`.
- Achievements are NOT visible to characters; they are a writer-facing tracking tool.
- Use this table to ensure milestone scenes are earned and memorable.

---

> Append new achievements as new rows. Use `Registry.md` to assign the next `ACH-` ID.
