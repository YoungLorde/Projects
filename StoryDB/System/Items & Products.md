## TABLE NOTES

- **Quantity:** Current known quantity in the story. Use `?` for unknown, `0` for planned-but-not-yet-produced, `∞` for effectively unlimited.
- **Unit:** The unit of measurement (stone, unit, reactor, ship, etc.).
- **Owner / Producer:** Who owns or produces it. May be a character, company, faction, or "Empire".
- **Source:** Where it came from: `System Shop`, `Mohamed's Invention`, `R&D`, `VR Research`, `Purchased`, `Loot`, `Gift`, etc.
- **Status:** Planned / Prototype / Active / Mass-Produced / Obsolete / Classified.
- When an item tier/version changes, add a new row (do not overwrite).
