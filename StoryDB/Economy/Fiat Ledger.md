## TABLE NOTES

- **Amount:** For transactions, use `+` or `-`. For valuations, use `= $X` or note the rank.
- **Balance / Valuation:** The resulting balance or company valuation after the event.
- **Entity:** The person or company involved.
- **USD→SP Conversion:** $100,000 USD = 1 SSP (per Canon default).
- When a valuation changes, add a new row rather than overwriting the old one.
