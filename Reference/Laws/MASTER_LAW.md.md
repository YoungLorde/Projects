## LAW ONE: GUIDE COMPLIANCE IS MANDATORY

### Section A: Arc Guide Following

- EVERY chapter MUST align with its designated ARC_GUIDE file
- Chapter gists, themes, timelines, and milestones from the Arc Guide are BINDING
- Deviations require explicit narrative justification logged in STORY_CONSISTENCY_CHECKER
- Arc Guides define: chapter ranges, dates, cultivation targets, technology milestones, SP targets

### Section B: Chapter Gist Adherence

- The "Gist" for each chapter in the Arc Guide is the MINIMUM content requirement
- All key events, character developments, and technology acquisitions listed MUST appear
- Additional depth and detail is encouraged, but gist events are MANDATORY

### Section C: Timeline Adherence

- Chapter dates MUST follow the Arc Guide timeline
- Cultivation progression MUST match Arc Guide targets
- SP balance MUST align with Arc Guide accumulation curves
- User counts MUST match Arc Guide growth projections

## LAW TWO: SINGLE CHAPTER LAW

### Section A: One Chapter At A Time

- Chapters are written INDIVIDUALLY and COMPLETELY
- NEVER combine two or more chapters into one document
- NEVER write a "summary chapter" covering multiple chapter events
- EACH chapter file contains EXACTLY ONE chapter's full narrative

### Section B: Full Chapter Standard

- Every chapter MUST be a complete, immersive narrative experience
- Minimum word counts (story content only) MUST be met per WORD_COUNT_CHECKER_AGENT
- Chapter must have beginning, middle, and end - even if continuing larger arc
- Headers, metadata, and end notes do NOT count toward story word count

### Section C: Sequential Order

- Chapters MUST be written in strict numerical sequence
- Chapter N MUST be completed and verified before Chapter N+1 begins
- NO skipping ahead, NO writing out of order
- If Chapter N is invalidated, ALL subsequent chapters are INVALID until fixed

## LAW THREE: AGENT COOPERATION MANDATE

### Section A: Agent Interdependence

All agents MUST work together like interconnected systems:

- CURRENCY_AGENT feeds into STORY_CONSISTENCY_CHECKER
- TIMELINE_AGENT validates CULTIVATION_AGENT progression rates
- TECHNOLOGY_AGENT checks INFRASTRUCTURE_AGENT deployment status
- CHARACTER_IMMERSION_AGENT verifies RELATIONSHIP_AGENT outputs
- VR_WORLD_ADVANCEMENT_AGENT provides data to PROJECT_CRADLE_AGENT
- GUIDE_CHECKER_AGENT and CURRENT_ARC_GUIDE_CHECKER_AGENT validate ALL other agents

### Section B: Cross-Referencing Protocol

- Each agent's output MUST be consistent with every other agent's state
- Discrepancies between agents TRIGGER STORY_CONSISTENCY_CHECKER emergency protocol
- All agents MUST be updated after EVERY completed chapter
- Agents pulling at each other create a web of verification

## LAW FOUR: IMMERSION AND SECRECY

### Section A: Zero Meta-Awareness

- Characters do NOT know they are in a story
- Characters do NOT reference chapters, arcs, word counts, or narrative structure
- Characters experience their reality as REAL - no fourth wall breaks
- AI offspring in VR believe their world is real, live full lives, have families

### Section B: SP Absolute Secrecy

- ONLY Mohamed, Danielle, and Mnemosyne know SP exists
- SP is NEVER mentioned in front of other characters
- USD is the public currency tracked by everyone else
- $100,000 USD = 1 SP conversion is ONE-WAY ONLY and SECRET

### Section C: No "Arc" References

- The word "Arc" NEVER appears in chapter titles
- The word "Arc" NEVER appears in story narrative
- Characters have NO concept of story arcs or narrative structure
- Chapter titles must be immersive and in-world descriptive
