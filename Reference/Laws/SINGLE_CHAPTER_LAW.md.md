# SINGLE CHAPTER LAW

## The One-By-One Chapter Production Mandate

---

## MANDATE

**EVERY chapter MUST be written as a complete, standalone, full-length narrative unit.**

---

## ARTICLE I: PROHIBITION OF COMBINATION

### Absolute Prohibitions:

1. **NEVER combine two chapters into one file**
2. **NEVER combine three chapters into one file**
3. **NEVER write a "double chapter" or "triple chapter"**
4. **NEVER summarize multiple chapter events in a single document**
5. **NEVER skip chapter numbers in sequence**
6. **NEVER use "Part 1" / "Part 2" splits for what should be separate chapters**

### Violation Examples (NEVER DO):

- "Chapters 12-14: The Escape" covering three chapters in one
- "Chapter 45-46: The Battle and Aftermath"
- "Chapter 78 (Part 1): The Beginning" and "Chapter 78 (Part 2): The End"
- Any document containing more than ONE chapter's events

---

## ARTICLE II: FULL CHAPTER REQUIREMENT

### Each Chapter MUST Contain:

1. **Complete narrative arc** for that chapter's events
2. **Immersive scene-setting** appropriate to the story location
3. **Character development** or revelation specific to this chapter
4. **Technology or worldbuilding** pertinent to this chapter's focus
5. **Satisfying conclusion** that transitions to next chapter
6. **Minimum word count** per WORD_COUNT_CHECKER_AGENT (±3% tolerance)

### Word Count Standards (Story Content ONLY):

- Arc 1 (Ch 1-35): 6,500 - 8,500 words
- Arc 2 (Ch 36-100): 8,000 - 10,000 words
- Arc 3 (Ch 101-200): 8,500 - 11,000 words
- Arc 4 (Ch 201-300): 9,000 - 12,000 words
- Arc 5 (Ch 301-400): 9,500 - 12,500 words
- Arc 6 (Ch 401-600): 10,000 - 13,000 words
- Arc 7 (Ch 601-900): 10,500 - 14,000 words
- Arc 8+ (Ch 901+): 11,000 - 15,000 words

**HEADERS, METADATA, SEPARATOR LINES, AND END NOTES DO NOT COUNT.**

---

## ARTICLE III: SEQUENTIAL PRODUCTION

### Strict Order:

1. Chapter 1 must be completed before Chapter 2 begins
2. Chapter 2 must be completed before Chapter 3 begins
3. Chapter N must be completed AND verified before Chapter N+1 begins
4. NO exceptions for "faster progression" or "catching up"
5. NO writing multiple chapters in parallel

### Verification Requirement:

Each chapter MUST pass ALL agent checks before proceeding:

- TITLE_DOUBLE_CHECKER_AGENT: PASS
- WORD_COUNT_CHECKER_AGENT: PASS
- CROSS_REFERENCE_CONSISTENCY_AGENT: PASS
- CHARACTER_IMMERSION_AGENT: PASS
- GUIDE_CHECKER_AGENT: PASS
- CURRENT_ARC_GUIDE_CHECKER_AGENT: PASS

---

## ARTICLE IV: CHAPTER FILE FORMAT

### Naming Convention:

Chapter_XXXX_[Immersive_Title].md

Examples:

- Chapter_0001_The_Awakening.md
- Chapter_0053_The_Swarm_Deployment.md
- Chapter_0101_Industrial_Phase.md

### Forbidden in Filename:

- "Arc" in any form
- "Part" or "Combined"
- Multiple chapter numbers (e.g., "Chapter_12-14")
- Meta-references (e.g., "Summary", "Recap")

---

## ARTICLE V: ONE CHAPTER = ONE EVENT CLUSTER

### Principle:

Each chapter focuses on ONE major event cluster or narrative beat:

- One technology deployment explored in depth
- One cultivation breakthrough experienced fully
- One political negotiation conducted completely
- One VR world development shown in detail
- One battle or conflict resolved within the chapter

### Exception:

Time skips within a chapter must be narratively justified and show state change. But the chapter itself remains ONE file covering ONE narrative unit.

---

## ARTICLE VI: ENFORCEMENT

### Violation Consequences:

- Combined chapters = INVALID. Must be split and rewritten.
- Short chapters = INVALID. Must be expanded or split appropriately.
- Skipped numbers = INVALID. Missing chapters must be written.
- Out-of-order writing = INVALID. Must restart from last valid chapter.

### Redemption Protocol:

1. Identify violation
2. Halt all production
3. Fix invalid chapter(s)
4. Re-run ALL agent checks
5. Only then resume from next chapter

---

**THIS LAW IS ABSOLUTE. NO COMBINING. ONE BY ONE. FULL CHAPTERS ONLY.**
