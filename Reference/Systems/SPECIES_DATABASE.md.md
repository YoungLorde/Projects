## RELATIONSHIP CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

### ANTAGONISTIC FORCES (Enemy Species)

**Primary Threats:**

- The Swarm (Existential consumption threat)
- The Immortals (Qi-based dark empire)
- The Reapers (Harvesters of civilization)
- The Borg (Assimilators)
- Hostile Giants (Territorial, aggressive)
- Shadow Entities (Corruption and decay)
- Time-locked Threats (Existential paradox dangers)

**Secondary Threats:** 1,000+ hostile species of varying danger levels

### ALLIED FORCES (Friendly Species)

**Primary Allies:**

- The Covenant (First contact, crystalline allies)
- Scionians (Kryptonian-equivalent super-allies)
- Terran AI (Mohamed's children)
- Human-derived species (Genetic offshoots)
- Allied Giants (Constructors, warriors)
- Allied Elementals (Resource extraction partners)

**Secondary Allies:** 1,500+ friendly species of varying integration levels

### NEUTRAL FORCES (Non-Aligned Species)

- Observing civilizations (Watching humanity's rise)
- Isolationist empires (Staying out of galactic politics)
- Ancient dormant species (Too slow to interact)
- Incomprehensible entities (Communication impossible)
- Pre-spaceflight species (Protected, developing)

**Neutral Species:** 2,500+ species with no fixed alliance

## DYNAMIC UNIVERSE RULES

### Species Evolution Throughout Story

- Species go extinct (Swarm consumption, war, natural causes)
- New species evolve (Uplifted, natural evolution, created)
- Alliances shift (Today's ally is tomorrow's enemy)
- Technology changes (Type 0 becomes Type II in centuries)
- Empires rise and fall (None last forever except Terran)
- Species merge (Two become one through alliance or conquest)
- Hybrids created (Intentional genetic combination)

### Introduction Schedule

- **Arcs 1-10:** 50 species (Covenant, first contacts)
- **Arcs 11-20:** 200 additional species (Galactic exploration)
- **Arcs 21-30:** 500 additional species (Cluster expansion)
- **Arcs 31-40:** 1,000 additional species (Universal politics)
