## PART 1 — THE SECRECY IMPERATIVE (ZERO TOLERANCE)

1. **ONLY Mohamed can perceive the System interface.** No other person, AI, or entity can see it.
2. **System Points (SP) are invisible.** No one but Mohamed (and later Danielle + Mnemosyne) may know SP exists.
3. **The Shop is hidden.** Mohamed's public cover: a self-taught genius inventor who "discovers" technologies through intensive private research. This is partially true (he does study to memorize purchased knowledge).
4. **SP→USD conversion is ONE-WAY.** USD converts INTO SP; SP NEVER converts back. Two parallel economies. (Note: source files give two conversion rates — see Resolved Canon Decisions below; the canonical rate is `$100,000 USD = 1 SSP`.)
5. **Any secrecy violation = chapter is INVALID and must be rewritten.**
6. **AI existence (Mnemosyne/Prometheus) is secret from human staff.** Inner-Circle humans know facility/tech but NOT the System/SP/cultivation source.
7. **No "Arc" references** in chapter titles or story narrative. No meta-awareness. Characters never know they are in a story. No fourth-wall breaks.
8. **No anachronisms. No deus ex machina** — the System has rules and costs.

---

## PART 2 — THE FOG OF DISCOVERY & INVENTION MANDATE

9. **Mohamed never "just knows" anything.** All knowledge must be purchased from the System Shop with SP. No insights gleaned without expenditure.
10. **The System provides theories; Mohamed engineers the applications.** Each breakthrough requires deliberate study and experimentation.
11. **Mohamed INVENTS core technology** — Mana Stones, Aether Stones, and the discovery of "Tiers"/"Levels" are his inventions/empirical findings, NOT System products.
12. **Replicators are purchased from the Shop but IMPROVED via R&D.** No instant perfect technology — show Mark-by-Mark iteration.
13. **VR-First Rule:** Major technology is first researched in time-dilated VR, then translated to reality (which requires physical materials/energy/time).

---

## PART 3 — PROGRESSION & PACING

14. **Logical, realistic progression.** Realism with magical elements. No sudden power-ups without foundation. Technology follows engineering principles.
15. **Slow, deliberate early progression (60 arcs × 50 chapters = 3,000 total):**
    - Ch 1-50: Software wealth building (drag this out)
    - Ch 51-100: Government conflicts & relocation
    - Ch 101-150: Third-world secret development
    - Ch 151-200: High-tech lab, weapons, robots, drones
    - Ch 201-250: Clean energy reactor research
    - Ch 251-300: Collider construction & Aether stone theory
    - Ch 301-350: First mana stone creation
    - **Empire thinking begins AFTER Chapter 400**
16. **VR research begins Ch 50+** (after Kenya facility). Constant background R&D. AI researchers Ch 100+. Human researchers use VR during sleep. Many iterations before real-world implementation.
17. **Technology timeline (each phase earned):** Software → relocation/security → hardware/manufacturing → energy → Aether/mystical → space/advanced.

## PART 4 — CHARACTER CANON

18. **Mohamed Vance:** 5'6" (167.6 cm), lean, dark complexion, short curly black hair, intense dark brown eyes. Born 1999-03-14 Nairobi; raised Louisville, KY. Age 26 at start. Kenyan-American. CNC machinist, graveyard shift, Keen American Built. Parents deceased (car accident, age 19). Expelled U of Louisville junior year (exposed grade-fixing). Calculating, patient, secretly romantic (private poetry journal). Fatal flaw: trusts his own judgment almost pathologically.
19. **Danielle Jones:** 5'1" (154.9 cm), petite, golden blonde hair, green eyes, athletic. Born 2003-08-03 Lexington, KY. Foster system. Self-taught programmer (age 11). Three associate degrees, no bachelor's. Data entry at Keen American Built when she meets Mohamed. Brash, irreverent, terrifyingly intelligent. **She NEVER learns the full truth of the System** — the series' most painful ongoing tension.
20. **Height differential: 5 inches, CONSTANT across all chapters.** Any scene with relative height description must maintain it.
21. **Mohamed's cultivation trait: PIONEER** — 1000× cultivation speed multiplier. Does NOT discover this until he first tests his rank. Danielle later inherits diluted pioneer traits.
22. **Mental state at start:** Alone, grieving, planning. Does NOT think about an empire. Focus = survival, wealth, protecting his tech. Forced into conflict by governments; relocates to develop in secret. Empire-building becomes necessary only later.

## PART 5 — STRUCTURE & QUALITY

23. **SINGLE CHAPTER LAW:** One chapter = one file = one narrative unit. NEVER combine chapters. NEVER skip numbers. Chapter N must be COMPLETE and VERIFIED before N+1 begins. Strict sequential order.
24. **Word count (story content only — headers/metadata/end-notes excluded):** 3,000–6,000 words per chapter. This is the canonical target band. Headers/metadata/separator lines/end notes NEVER count. The writer may pick a target within this band per chapter; verify it stays within the band unless the user explicitly overrides.
25. **Chapter file naming:** `Chapter N - [Immersive Title].md` (matching the existing files in `Chapters/`). Title must be immersive/in-world; NO "Arc", "Part", "Combined", "Summary", or "Recap".
26. **Chapter structure:** Opening (hook, scene-set, character state, chapter goal) → Middle (3-5 distinct scenes) → Ending (climax/resolution, consequence, hook for next, REQUIRED date stamp).
27. **Required header block** at top of each chapter: Date, Arc, Location, Cultivation (Mohamed + Danielle), Lifespan, SP Balance, Passive SP/hr, Total Users, Key Characters, Technologies Introduced, Karma Events Seeded, Word Count Target.
28. **Dialogue voices:** Mohamed = modern, professional, occasionally humorous, not stiff, quiet/intense. Danielle = sharp, direct, witty, mischievous, technical jargon when apt. Every line reveals character or advances plot.
29. **Writing style (NON-ROBOTIC):** Varied sentence length. Natural dialogue tags. Show-don't-tell. Occasional fragments. Contractions. Hesitations/interruptions/incomplete thoughts. Sensory priority (temperature, smell, texture). Webnovel pacing: hook every 2-3 paragraphs, cliffhanger at chapter end, never summarize what was just shown. Avoid: repetitive structures, adverb stacking, formulaic transitions, robotic emotion, philosophical rambling, meta-commentary.
30. **IMMERSION RULE:** The Empire DOMINATES — show victories, power, expansion, wealth, ascending humanity. No hand-wringing philosophical debates about power.

## PART 6 — THE EMPIRE CORE & DELEGATION

31. **Three pillars:** Mohamed (Emperor, System Holder, Cultivation Apex), Danielle (Empress, Technical Genius, Dual-Cultivation Partner), The AIs (Mnemosyne, Prometheus, descendants — a governing pillar EQUAL to the human rulers).
32. **Mohamed is NOT the only cultivator.** Humanity produces thousands of geniuses, champions, elite cultivators. Mohamed delegates EVERYTHING to the best people. The Empire becomes MORE efficient as it grows.
33. **AI autonomy progresses** from tools → assistants → junior partners → senior partners → governors → architects → equals → successors. (Full matrix in `Reference/Agents/AI_AUTONOMY_AGENT.md.md`.)

---

## PART 7 — AETHER (HUMANITY'S EDGE)

34. **Aether** is humanity-exclusive, discovered/invented by Mohamed. Superior to Mana (alien races) and Qi (Immortals). Enables cross-rank combat advantage. The Empire protects this advantage absolutely; all theft attempts fail.
35. **Aether Stones** are 9999× denser than Mana Stones, invented by Mohamed, become the galactic currency standard. Empire holds monopoly on Aether Stones and Replicators.
