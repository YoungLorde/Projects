## CONSISTENCY RULES

### 1. Capacity Limits

Facility cannot exceed reasonable expansion rates  
Maximum 1 new level per 10 chapters (early phase)  
Staff growth limited by living quarters and logistics

### 2. Resource Constraints

All materials must have documented source  
Power generation must match consumption + growth  
Food/water must support staff count

### 3. Construction Time

Major projects: 5-20 chapters  
Infrastructure upgrades: 2-5 chapters  
Emergency repairs: Immediate  
VR development: Doesn't count (virtual time)

### 4. Logistical Reality

Supplies don't appear instantly  
Transport takes time (unless teleportation available)  
Money laundering has limits and risks  
Staff recruitment takes time and vetting

## VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Before writing infrastructure changes:

- Is the expansion rate realistic?
- Are resources available for construction?
- Can logistics support the change?
- Does power generation support new demand?
- Is staff count appropriate for facility size?
- Are there security implications?
- Does the cover story support the change?
