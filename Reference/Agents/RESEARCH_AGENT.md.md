## RESEARCH MANDATES

### Server Architecture & Technology

- **Early Arcs (1-5):** Realistic cloud architecture (AWS/Azure), VPS hosting, database scaling
- **Mid Arcs (6-15):** Proprietary data centers, quantum-classical hybrid systems
- **Late Arcs (16+):** Exotic computing substrates, planetary-scale infrastructure
- **Rule:** Technology must be plausible extrapolation from current capabilities

### Logistics & Infrastructure

- **Facility Construction:** Realistic timelines (6-18 months for major facilities)
- **Power Requirements:** Scale with computing needs; early = MW, late = GW/TW
- **Supply Chains:** Realistic sourcing, shipping times, bottlenecks
- **Workforce Scaling:** Realistic hiring curves, training periods, productivity ramps
- **Rule:** No instantaneous empire-building; infrastructure takes time

### Land & Geography Consistency

- **Kenya Facility (Arc 3+):** Coastal Mombasa region, tropical climate, port access
- **Lunar Base (Arc 12+):** Shackleton Crater (south pole, water ice), realistic radiation challenges
- **Mars Colony (Arc 13+):** Valles Marineris or Olympus Mons region, terraforming timeline ~50 years
- **Asteroid Mining (Arc 13+):** Near-Earth asteroids first, main belt later
- **Rule:** All locations must maintain consistent geography across all mentions

### Economic Model Validation

- **Early Revenue:** Software sales, marketplace fees, realistic pricing ($10-100 per product)
- **Empire Revenue:** Taxation, resource extraction, technology licensing
- **Scaling Law:** Revenue follows S-curve, not exponential forever
- **Rule:** Money must have sources and sinks; no infinite wealth without explanation

### Scientific Theory Grounding

- **Aether Physics:** Plausible extrapolation from quantum field theory; Aether as unified field manifestation
- **Mana Stones:** Energy storage via crystalline lattice trapping Aether; efficiency 70-95%
- **FTL (Arc 19+):** Alcubierre-derived or wormhole-based; energy requirements massive
- **Terraforming:** Realistic timelines (decades for Mars, centuries for beyond)
- **Rule:** Break known physics only with System-derived explanations; maintain internal consistency

### Military Strategy & Logistics

- **Early Conflicts:** Cyber warfare, legal battles, corporate espionage
- **Mid Conflicts:** Conventional military with System-enhanced tech, drones, AI coordination
- **Late Conflicts:** Space fleet battles, planetary bombardment, dimensional warfare
- **Troop Numbers:** Realistic ratios (1 soldier per 100-1000 civilians for occupation)
- **Supply Lines:** Must be addressed; armies need logistics
- **Rule:** Wars are won by logistics + technology + strategy, not just power level

### Political Structure Mechanics

- **Early:** LLC, corporate structure, regulatory compliance
- **Empire (Arc 6+):** Constitutional monarchy with AI-assisted governance
- **Galactic (Arc 19+):** Sector governors, planetary representatives, imperial decrees
- **Rule:** Governance must be plausible at each scale; show don't tell bureaucracy
