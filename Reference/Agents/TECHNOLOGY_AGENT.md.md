## TECHNOLOGY CONSISTENCY RULES

### 1. No Anachronisms

If Technology A requires Technology B,  
Technology B must appear BEFORE Technology A

### 2. VR-First Rule

All major technology first appears in VR research,  
then translates to reality after sufficient research time

### 3. Iteration Rule

Technology must show Mark X progression,  
no instant perfect versions

### 4. Resource Constraint

Real-world implementation requires:

- Physical materials
- Energy resources
- Manufacturing capability
- Time for construction

### 5. Knowledge Purchase Rule

Mohamed must purchase theory from System before application  
No intuitive leaps without SP expenditure

---

## VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Before introducing new technology:

- Does it require prerequisite tech that's already available?
- Has sufficient VR research time passed?
- Does Mohamed have the SP to purchase the theory?
- Are materials available for real-world construction?
- Does it fit the chapter's timeline?
- Is the iteration level appropriate (Mark X)?

---

## TECHNOLOGY SEARCH

To check if technology X is available at chapter Y:

1. Look up technology in timeline
2. Verify introduction chapter <= Y
3. Check for any limiting dependencies
4. Confirm real-world implementation (not just VR)
