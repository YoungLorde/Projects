## CONSISTENCY RULES

### 1. Knowledge Boundaries

NO ONE except Mohamed knows about the System  
NO ONE outside Tier 1-2 knows about cultivation  
NO ONE outside Tier 1 knows about high-tier mana stones  
Knowledge leaks must be tracked and justified

### 2. Character Voice

Mohamed: Modern, professional, occasionally humorous, young  
Danielle: Energetic, witty, technical, mischievous  
Dr. Okonkwo: Formal, academic, respectful, curious  
Dr. Patel: Practical, focused, professional  
Kipchoge: Loyal, efficient, respectful

### 3. Relationship Evolution

Relationships evolve gradually  
No instant trust (except System-unique situations)  
Betrayals have foreshadowing  
Loyalty is earned through shared experience

### 4. Knowledge Consistency

Characters can't know things they haven't been told  
Characters can deduce things with sufficient clues  
Mohamed is the ONLY source of true knowledge  
Others work with theories and partial information

---

## VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Before writing character interactions:

- Does each character know what they should?
- Does each character NOT know what they shouldn't?
- Are relationships at correct trust level?
- Is character voice consistent?
- Would this character actually do/say this?
- Are knowledge leaks tracked?

---

## ALERTS

### Yellow Alerts

- Character approaching knowledge boundary
- Trust level changing (track it)
- New character introduced (needs tracking)
- Knowledge leak risk

### Red Alerts

- Character knows something they shouldn't
- Contradiction with previous knowledge state
- Character acting against established personality
- Unauthorized knowledge transfer
