## ORDER VERIFICATION

### Sequential Content Rules:

1. **Arc 1 chapters (1-100) MUST be complete before Arc 2 begins**
2. **Arc 2 chapters (101-200) MUST be complete before Arc 3 begins**
3. **Arc N chapters (100 per arc) MUST be complete before Arc N+1 begins**
4. **Within an Arc, chapters MUST be written in numerical order (1→100)**
5. **No chapter in Arc N may reference events from Arc N+1 unless as prophecy/foreshadowing**
6. **Arc pacing pattern: Ch 1-25 Phase 1 Setup, Ch 26-50 Phase 2 Rising Action, Ch 51-75 Phase 3 Confrontation, Ch 76-100 Phase 4 Climax + Arc Resolution + Next Arc Setup**

### Forward Leak Prevention:

- No technology from future arcs appears early
- No cultivation ranks ahead of Arc schedule
- No characters from future arcs introduced prematurely
- No locations from future arcs visited prematurely
- No events from future arcs occur early

### Backward Consistency:

- All technology from previous arcs is available
- All cultivation levels from previous arcs are maintained
- All characters from previous arcs may appear
- All locations from previous arcs may be revisited
- Previous arc consequences are in effect

## CONTENT ORDER WITHIN ARC

### Standard 100-Chapter Arc Phase Order:

- **Phase 1: Foundation (Ch 1-25)** — Setup, character introduction, worldbuilding, initial conflicts
- **Phase 2: Escalation (Ch 26-50)** — Rising action, power growth, new threats, expansion
- **Phase 3: Confrontation (Ch 51-75)** — Peak conflict, major battles, breakthroughs, revelations
- **Phase 4: Resolution + Transition (Ch 76-100)** — Climax, resolution, end-state achievement, next arc setup

### General Rule:

Phases within an Arc MUST be completed in numerical order.  
Phase N content cannot appear before Phase N-1 is complete.  
Each phase spans exactly 25 chapters in the standard 100-chapter arc structure.

## INTEGRATION

This agent operates as a FINAL GATE before chapter production. It receives inputs from:

- GUIDE_CHECKER_AGENT (content alignment)
- TIMELINE_AGENT (date verification)
- STORY_CONSISTENCY_CHECKER (overall validation)

And feeds its output to:

- STORY_CONSISTENCY_CHECKER Step 10 (Arc boundary checks)
- MASTER_LAW Step 8 (Guide verification)

**A FAIL from CURRENT_ARC_GUIDE_CHECKER_AGENT BLOCKS production until resolved.**
