## WORLD CONSISTENCY RULES

### 1. Realistic Politics

Nations act in self-interest  
No instant world unity  
Resistance proportional to power  
Small nations join first  
Major powers negotiate

### 2. Economic Realism

Technology disruption causes short-term chaos  
Markets adjust over time  
Winners and losers from change  
Social programs needed for transition

### 3. Social Dynamics

Public opinion shifts gradually  
Fear of change is real  
Hope drives acceptance  
Generational differences in adaptation

### 4. Media Reality

Sensationalism sells  
Conspiracy theories always exist  
Truth eventually emerges  
Narrative control is limited

## VALIDATION CHECKLIST

Before writing world events:

- Is the political reaction realistic?
- Does economic impact make sense?
- Would public opinion actually change this way?
- Are there unintended consequences?
- Does the timeline allow for adjustment?
- Have all major powers been considered?
