# The Game System — Core Reference

## Nature
The Game System is a **cold, mechanical process**. It has no personality, no emotions, no agenda. It does not communicate conversationally. It delivers notifications in rigid, formatted blocks. It does not care about fairness, morality, or individual outcomes. It simply *is*.

Think of it as gravity — it applies equally, without thought, without mercy.

## System Notifications Format
```
+================================================+
           SYSTEM NOTIFICATION
+================================================+
  [Content — stats, rewards, warnings, updates]
+================================================+
```

## Terminology
- **Travellers**: All players bonded to a Vehicle Core. The universal term across all species and universes.
- **Wanderers**: Travellers who have lost their Vehicle Core (stolen, destroyed, or forcibly extracted). Their Soul Vehicle dematerializes permanently. They retain their personal stats but lose vehicle access, tier bonuses, and mobile base functionality. The core extraction trauma often triggers **Phantom Travelling** — compulsive wandering. Wanderers are considered vulnerable — many factions hunt them.
- **Vehicle Core**: The crystalline nexus bonded to every Traveller's soul. Merges into the body upon bonding. Determines Soul Vehicle access, vehicle tier, upgrade capacity, and pocket dimension access. Each core has a tier rating.
- **Soul Vehicle**: The vehicle summoned through a bonded Vehicle Core. Chosen at initial activation from three domain options (land/navy/air for humans). Soul bound, summonable, self-repairing, upgradeable, and growable with tier advancement.

## Vehicle Core & Soul Vehicle Mechanics
- Every Traveller receives a Vehicle Core upon System activation
- The core bonds to the Traveller's **soul** — it merges into their body, not carried physically
- After Core Synchronization, the System presents **three starter Soul Vehicle choices** based on species
- **Human choices:** Land Vehicle (sedan/truck/van), Navy Vehicle (speedboat/yacht), Air Vehicle (helicopter/plane)
- **The choice is PERMANENT** — the vehicle's domain (land/navy/air) cannot be changed
- Cross-domain capability (e.g., land vehicle gaining flight) becomes available via upgrades at Tier 3+
- Soul Vehicles are **summonable** — summoned/dismissed at will, no RP cost, 3s to summon, 1s to dismiss
- Soul Vehicles are **soul bound** — only the bonded Traveller can operate them
- Soul Vehicles **self-repair** at 1% hull/hour using ambient System energy
- If destroyed (0 HP), cannot be resummoned for **24 hours** while the core regenerates it
- Core tiers range from Tier 0 (Starter) upward with no known ceiling
- **Alien species have different Soul Vehicle options** reflecting their biology and culture (see vehicle_core_mechanics.yaml)
- **Stolen Core Rules:**
  - If the stolen core's tier matches the thief's vehicle tier → **3-5 free upgrades within that tier**
  - If the stolen core's tier is **lower** than the thief's → **converted to RP** (amount scales with tier difference)
  - If the stolen core's tier is **higher** than the thief's → **cannot be absorbed** (incompatible energy density)
  - **Exception: Mohamed Vance** — His hidden secondary system bypasses tier restrictions, allowing absorption of any core regardless of tier

## Mohamed's Dual System
Mohamed possesses two overlapping systems:

1. **Primary System (Game System)**: The same cold, mechanical system every living creature has. Governs stats, levels, notifications, Vehicle Core bonding, and standard progression.
2. **Secondary System (Hidden Overlay)**: An invasive system layered on top of the Game System. The Game System **cannot detect it**. This overlay grants:
   - **RP Conversion**: The ability to convert any material, energy, or resource into Replication Points
   - **Core Bypass**: Can absorb Vehicle Cores of any tier
   - **System Sight**: Can see hidden System data that normal Travellers cannot access
   - The secondary system has its own notification format (subtle, integrated into the primary)

The secondary system's origin is one of the novel's central mysteries.

## What the System Governs
- Stat allocation and tracking (STR, AGI, END, INT, PER, LCK, SYS)
- Level progression
- RP rewards for kills, discoveries, quests, and achievements
- Vehicle Core bonding and tier management
- Dungeon generation and management
- Monster spawning and tier assignment
- Skill and ability unlocks
- Zone difficulty ratings
- Death penalties (varies by zone and tier)

## What the System Does NOT Do
- Communicate conversationally
- Show favoritism or mercy
- Explain its own rules (Travellers learn through experience and shared knowledge)
- Have administrators, authorities, or governing bodies
- Provide customer support
- Make exceptions (except where exploits like Mohamed's exist)

## Universe Coverage
- The Game System exists across **all known universes**
- Over **60% of the universe remains unexplored and unknown**
- Different universes may have slightly different System manifestations (different UI, different stat names) but the underlying mechanics are identical
- Hidden worlds and pocket dimensions exist outside normal System tracking
