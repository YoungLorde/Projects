# HUMAN FACTIONS

## The Convoy
- **Philosophy:** Safety in numbers, democratic governance, roaming alliance
- **Strength:** 300+ players
- **Leadership:** Elected council (rotates)
- **Vehicle Average:** Tier 3-6
- **Territory:** Mobile — no fixed territory
- **Mohamed's Assessment:** "A herd waiting to be slaughtered"
- **Strengths:** Numbers, shared resources, morale
- **Weaknesses:** Slow-moving, visible, democratic process slows decisions
- **Status:** Active, growing

## The Iron Compact
- **Philosophy:** Militaristic, rule through strength, tax weaker players
- **Strength:** 150+ elite players
- **Leadership:** Commander Hale (Level ~40, Tier 8)
- **Vehicle Average:** Tier 5-10
- **Territory:** Controls The Sprawl and surrounding zones
- **Mohamed's Assessment:** "Effective but eventually unsustainable"
- **Strengths:** Discipline, firepower, established territory
- **Weaknesses:** Creates enemies through oppression, rigid hierarchy
- **Status:** Active, antagonistic to Mohamed

## The Scholars
- **Philosophy:** Research the Game's origins and rules
- **Strength:** ~40 players
- **Leadership:** Dr. Lin (former physicist)
- **Vehicle Average:** Tier 2-5
- **Territory:** Hidden research bunker
- **Mohamed's Assessment:** "Potentially useful — information is power"
- **Strengths:** Knowledge, analytical capability, unique insights
- **Weaknesses:** Militarily weak, dependent on others for protection
- **Status:** Active, cautiously neutral

## The Bloodless
- **Philosophy:** Pragmatic alliance with Vampire players
- **Strength:** 80+ players + Sanguis Court backing
- **Leadership:** Shared human-vampire council
- **Vehicle Average:** Tier 4-8 (vampire vehicles much higher)
- **Strengths:** Vampire protection, access to ancient knowledge
- **Weaknesses:** Viewed as traitors by other humans, dependent on vampires
- **Status:** Active, growing influence

## The Remnant
- **Philosophy:** Preserve human culture and knowledge
- **Strength:** 200+ players
- **Leadership:** Archive Council
- **Vehicle Average:** Tier 2-4
- **Territory:** Fortified library/museum complex
- **Strengths:** Institutional knowledge, cultural cohesion
- **Weaknesses:** Sentimental attachment to pre-Game world, limited combat
- **Status:** Active, isolationist

## Free Agents
- **Philosophy:** No faction allegiance
- **Strength:** Thousands of individual players
- **Mohamed's Category:** Yes
- **Strengths:** Freedom, unpredictability, no political obligations
- **Weaknesses:** No backup, no infrastructure, no information networks
