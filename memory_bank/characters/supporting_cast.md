# SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

## Selia Drath — Veteran Alien Player
- **Species:** Drathian (7-limbed, silicon-based, semi-translucent, color-shifting)
- **Game Tenure:** 340 years
- **Vehicle:** Tier 180 crystal-lattice vehicle (living architecture)
- **Motivation:** Collects interesting phenomena — Mohamed is a phenomenon
- **Personality:** Pragmatic, patient, non-emotional assessment
- **Speech Pattern:** Flowery, complex sentences, references to vast timescales
- **Introduced:** Arc 5 (~Ch. 160)
- **Relationship to Mohamed:** Threat, resource, puzzle simultaneously

## Jarvis Cole — Human Player
- **Background:** Former logistics manager
- **Survival Strategy:** Intelligence over strength
- **Vehicle:** Tier 8 at introduction, steadily upgrades
- **Specialty:** Information networks, trading, knowing where everything is
- **Personality:** Casual, efficient, pragmatic
- **Speech Pattern:** Logistics jargon, "the numbers say"
- **Introduced:** Arc 2 (~Ch. 50)
- **Relationship to Mohamed:** Useful information source, watched constantly

## Mira Vasq — The Rival
- **Background:** Natural leader, community builder
- **Strategy:** Opposite of Mohamed — alliances, community, collective strength
- **Vehicle:** Tier 12 at introduction, grows quickly (faction resources)
- **Faction:** Leads a growing, successful human community
- **Personality:** Warm, direct, leadership cadence
- **Speech Pattern:** Inclusive language ("we," "our," "together")
- **Introduced:** Arc 4 (~Ch. 130)
- **Relationship to Mohamed:** Philosophical mirror — mutual respect, irreconcilable differences

## Vor'Keth — Vampire Delegate
- **Species:** Vampire (Sanguis Court)
- **Game Tenure:** 600+ years
- **Role:** Diplomat and manipulator of extraordinary skill
- **Personality:** Formal, archaic, patient, collects debts
- **Speech Pattern:** "It would be... unfortunate," implied threats
- **Introduced:** Arc 5 (~Ch. 170)
- **Relationship to Mohamed:** Chess grandmaster observing an interesting pawn

## Ren — The Betrayer
- **Background:** Ordinary human player, genuinely kind
- **Role:** Mohamed's first attempt at trust — and first betrayal
- **Betrayal:** Sells Mohamed's location to Iron Compact for protection
- **Fate:** Hunted by Mohamed in Arc 4
- **Introduced:** Arc 3 (~Ch. 70)
- **Significance:** Crystallizes Mohamed's "trust no one" philosophy

## Kara — The Stealth Survivor
- **Background:** Survived through stealth and evasion
- **Vehicle:** Low tier, heavily camouflaged
- **Personality:** Quiet, observant, risk-averse
- **Introduced:** Arc 2 (~Ch. 28)
- **Significance:** Minor recurring character, contrast to Mohamed's aggressive approach
