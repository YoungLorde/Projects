# EARTH ZONES — LOCATION DATABASE

## Mohamed's Starting Region

### The Sprawl
- **Former Identity:** Major metropolitan area
- **Current State:** Collapsed high-rises, flooded streets, vegetation overgrowth
- **Resources:** Scrap metal, electronics, preserved food
- **Threats:** Zombie hordes, player gangs, feral predators
- **Controlled By:** Iron Compact (contested)
- **Danger Level:** Moderate-High

### The Fracture
- **Former Identity:** Highway system
- **Current State:** Canyon-like segments from tectonic shifts
- **Resources:** Vehicle parts, fuel reserves
- **Threats:** Ambush predators, player bandits
- **Controlled By:** Uncontrolled
- **Danger Level:** High

### The Dead Mall
- **Former Identity:** Shopping complex
- **Current State:** Structurally intact, dark interior, multiple levels
- **Resources:** Preserved goods, clothing, tools, hidden tech
- **Threats:** Stage 3-4 zombies, territorial players
- **Controlled By:** Contested
- **Danger Level:** High

### The Crater Fields
- **Former Identity:** Suburban neighborhoods
- **Current State:** Crater-pocked landscape from dimensional debris
- **Resources:** Alien mineral deposits, dimensional fragments
- **Threats:** Dimensional fauna, radiation zones
- **Controlled By:** Uncontrolled
- **Danger Level:** Extreme

### The Underground
- **Former Identity:** Subway and sewer system
- **Current State:** Partially flooded extensive tunnel network
- **Resources:** Rare minerals, preserved pre-Game technology
- **Threats:** Stage 5 zombies, unknown entities
- **Controlled By:** Unknown
- **Danger Level:** Extreme

### The Tower
- **Former Identity:** Lone surviving skyscraper
- **Current State:** Partially intact, visible for kilometers
- **Resources:** Communications equipment, vantage point
- **Threats:** Multi-faction contested
- **Controlled By:** Rotating control
- **Danger Level:** Variable (political)

## Earth — Global Changes
- Geography warped — mountains where plains were, chasms splitting highways
- Sky cycles unnatural colors on 37-hour rotation
- Multiple wrong-angle moons (dimensional debris)
- Dimensional tears bleed light from other universes
- System Storms disable vehicles temporarily
- Water contaminated with System-active particles
- Time flows inconsistently in some zones
