# Chapter 1: Day Zero

## The Awakening

The sky cracked.

Not like thunder—Mohamed Vance had heard thunder plenty of times growing up in the Bronx, the kind that rattled windowpanes and sent bodega cats scrambling under shelves. This was different. This was the sound of reality folding in on itself, a noise that bypassed the ears entirely and vibrated somewhere behind his eyes, in the marrow of his bones, in the spaces between his thoughts.

He was twenty-four years old, standing in the middle of a Tuesday afternoon, holding a bag of groceries that included exactly three cans of black beans, a loaf of bread, and a bottle of hot sauce he'd been debating whether he could afford. The hot sauce won. It always did.

Then the sky cracked, and money stopped mattering.

The fracture spread across the heavens like a windshield hit by a stone at highway speed—jagged lines of impossible white light splitting the blue into geometric shards. People on the street froze. Cars stopped. Someone screamed, but the sound was swallowed by the low, subsonic hum that seemed to emanate from everywhere and nowhere.

Mohamed dropped the groceries. The hot sauce shattered on the sidewalk, and he would later think about that—how the last normal thing he ever did was waste four dollars and thirty-seven cents on concrete.

**[SYSTEM INITIALIZING]**

The words appeared directly in his field of vision. Not on a screen, not projected onto a surface—they simply *existed* in his perception, crisp white text floating against the chaos of the fracturing sky.

**[UNIVERSAL CONVERSION PROTOCOL ENGAGED]**
**[DESIGNATION: TRAVELLER]**
**[VEHICLE CORE: BINDING IN PROGRESS...]**

His chest burned. Not painfully at first—more like the warmth of swallowed whiskey spreading through his sternum. Then it intensified, became a white-hot brand pressed against the inside of his ribcage, and Mohamed dropped to his knees on the hot sauce-stained sidewalk, gasping.

Around him, everyone else was doing the same.

Every single person on the street—the woman in the business suit, the old man with the walker, the teenager on the bicycle, the truck driver who'd stopped mid-turn—all of them were clutching their chests, eyes wide, mouths open in silent screams as something fundamental about their existence was rewritten.

**[VEHICLE CORE: BOUND]**
**[TIER: 0]**
**[RP BALANCE: 0]**
**[STATUS: UNAWAKENED]**

The burning stopped as suddenly as it had started. Mohamed stayed on his knees, breathing hard, sweat running down his face despite the October chill. The text in his vision pulsed once, twice, then stabilized into something he would come to know as intimately as his own heartbeat.

**[SYSTEM INTERFACE]**
**NAME:** Mohamed Vance
**LEVEL:** 1
**RANK:** Unranked (Scavenger)
**STATS:**
- STR: 5
- AGI: 4
- VIT: 4
- INT: 6
- WIS: 3
- LCK: 7
- SYS: 1

**RP BALANCE:** 0
**RP MULTIPLIER:** 1.0x
**ASSIMILATION STAGE:** Interface Sync (Stage 0)

**VEHICLE CORE:** Tier 0 (Dormant)
**SOUL VEHICLE:** Not Summoned

Mohamed stared at the floating interface, his mind struggling to process what he was seeing. Stats. Like a game. Like one of those RPGs he'd played as a kid on borrowed consoles, except this was *real*, etched into his reality with the same certainty as gravity.

LCK: 7. Higher than anything else. He almost laughed. Lucky Mohamed, they'd called him back in the neighborhood. The kid who found twenty-dollar bills in storm drains, who dodged stray bullets by deciding to tie his shoe at exactly the right moment, who won scratch-offs just often enough to keep buying them.

Lucky Mohamed, who was now kneeling in hot sauce and broken glass while the world ended.

**[ATTENTION: VEHICLE CORE ACTIVATION REQUIRED]**
**[CHOOSE YOUR SOUL VEHICLE DOMAIN:]**

**1. LAND VEHICLE** — *Standard Starter: Mini Van*
   - Interior Space: 12m² (expandable)
   - Base Speed: 120 km/h
   - Fuel: RP-Sustained
   - Special: Ground Stability Bonus

**2. NAVAL VEHICLE** — *Standard Starter: Patrol Boat*
   - Interior Space: 15m² (expandable)
   - Base Speed: 45 knots
   - Fuel: RP-Sustained
   - Special: Aquatic Environment Bonus

**3. AIR VEHICLE** — *Standard Starter: Light Shuttle*
   - Interior Space: 8m² (expandable)
   - Base Speed: 200 km/h
   - Fuel: RP-Sustained
   - Special: Altitude Advantage Bonus

**[WARNING: THIS CHOICE IS PERMANENT. YOUR SOUL VEHICLE IS BOUND TO YOUR EXISTENCE.]**

Mohamed's hand trembled as he reached toward the floating text. Around him, he could see others doing the same—reaching toward invisible menus that only they could see, making choices that would define the rest of their lives.

*Land*, he thought. Not because it was the best choice—he had no idea what the best choice was. But because he was from the Bronx, because concrete and asphalt were the only ground he'd ever known, because when the world breaks apart, you want something solid under your wheels.

**[SELECTION: LAND VEHICLE — MINI VAN]**
**[SOUL VEHICLE BOUND]**
**[VEHICLE MAY BE SUMMONED AT WILL]**

The moment he made the choice, something shifted inside him. The Vehicle Core in his chest pulsed once—warm, alive, *waiting*—and he understood, without being told, that he could call it forth whenever he wanted. His vehicle. His domain. His mobile fortress in whatever this new world was going to be.

Then the second notification appeared—the one that no one else received.

It flickered at the edge of his perception, almost hidden behind the System interface, like a parasite riding on the back of a host. A second layer of text, rendered in a slightly different shade of white that the System's own interface couldn't seem to detect.

**[SECONDARY SYSTEM DETECTED]**
**[OVERLAY: ACTIVE]**
**[RP CONVERSION MODULE: ONLINE]**
**[NOTE: Primary System cannot detect this interface.]**

Mohamed's blood went cold. He looked around. No one else was reacting to a second set of notifications. No one else had a ghost system riding their real one.

**[RP CONVERSION ABILITY UNLOCKED]**
**[You may convert ANY resource, material, or energy into RP at enhanced rates.]**
**[Current Conversion Efficiency: 1.2x (scales with level)]**
**[This ability is invisible to the Primary System and all System-based detection methods.]**

He closed his eyes. Opened them. The second interface was still there, nestled behind the first like a shadow.

He didn't know what it meant. He didn't know why he had it. He didn't know if it was a gift or a curse or a glitch in whatever cosmic machinery had just rewritten the rules of existence.

But Mohamed Vance had grown up in the Bronx. He'd learned early that when life hands you something—anything—you don't ask questions. You pocket it, you keep your mouth shut, and you figure out how to use it before someone figures out you have it.

The sky finished cracking. The shards of blue dissolved into a vast, star-filled expanse that had no business being visible at 2:47 PM on a Tuesday afternoon. And somewhere in the distance, the first monsters began to emerge.

Mohamed stood up, brushed the broken glass from his knees, and summoned his vehicle.

The Mini Van materialized around him—not appearing from nothing, but *unfolding* from the Vehicle Core in his chest like a origami structure expanding in reverse. One moment he was standing on a broken sidewalk. The next, he was inside a space that was simultaneously twelve square meters and infinite with potential.

The interior was sparse. Basic. A driver's seat, a small cargo area, bare metal walls. But the System interface told him what it could become—internal upgrades, expansions, facilities, weapons systems. All purchasable. All upgradable. All requiring one thing:

RP.

Reality Points. The currency of the new world. Earned by killing monsters, completing objectives, trading, salvaging—or, in Mohamed's case, converting literally anything into them.

He looked at the broken bottle of hot sauce still staining the sidewalk outside his van.

**[RP CONVERSION: Hot Sauce Remnants]**
**[Material Value: Negligible]**
**[RP Yield: 0.3 RP]**
**[Convert? Y/N]**

Mohamed smiled. It wasn't much. But it was a start.

**[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]**

> ATTENTION ALL TRAVELLERS: The Conversion has begun. Your Vehicle Core is your lifeline. Protect it. Feed it. Evolve it. The universe is vast, dangerous, and largely unknown. Over 60% of explored space remains unmapped. Survival is not guaranteed. The System provides. The System does not protect.

> Current Global Traveller Count: 8,247,391,002
> Current Wanderer Count: 0
> Average Survival Rate (Projected): 23.7%

Twenty-three point seven percent. Out of eight billion people, the System expected less than a quarter to survive.

Mohamed looked at his stats again. STR 5, AGI 4, VIT 4, INT 6, WIS 3, LCK 7, SYS 1. Below average in most categories. Above average in luck.

He looked at his hidden second System, the ghost interface that promised infinite conversion potential.

He looked at the stars that now filled the sky where blue had been, at the shadows moving between the buildings, at the distant sounds of chaos and terror and the first screams of people encountering whatever had emerged from the cracks in reality.

"Alright," Mohamed said to no one. To everyone. To the cold, mechanical System that had just upended eight billion lives without a shred of empathy or explanation. "Let's see what we're working with."

He put the van in drive.

[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]: Day Zero has begun. Mohamed Vance, Traveller #4,891,337,204, has chosen the Land Vehicle domain. Vehicle Core: Tier 0. RP Balance: 0.3. Secondary System: Active. The Variable stirs.

**— End of Chapter 1 —**
